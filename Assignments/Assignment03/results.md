# Network Location Analysis

## What I Wanted to See
I wanted to compare YouTube's famous network fusion with decentralized Twitter alternatives.

## What Actually Happened
Since I'm in New York, everything has very good CDN acceleration here. So they all show up as highly concentrated in New York.

## Results
- **YouTube, X.com, Nitter**: Mostly New York locations
- **Reddit, Discord**: More diverse locations

## Why Reddit and Discord Are Different
They have more embedded content, so the results are more varied.

## Simple Conclusion
My location in New York made everything look centralized. Only platforms with lots of embedded content showed geographic diversity.

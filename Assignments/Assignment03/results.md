### Beginning
I wanted to compare YouTube's famous network fusion with cdn, compare Twitter with its decentralized version.

### Unexpected
Cause its New York, everything has very good CDN acceleration here. So they all show up as highly concentrated IPs in New York.

- **YouTube, X.com, Nitter**: Mostly New York locations
- **Reddit, Discord**: More diverse locations

- **Reddit and Discord Are Different**:

    They have more embedded content, so the results are more varied.

### Simple Conclusion
My location in New York made everything look centralized. Only platforms with lots of embedded content showed geographic diversity.
